User guide

Nutka searches YouTube Music, lets you listen first and saves the track as an mp3 with cover art and tags. It also downloads whole albums and playlists from Spotify. This page explains everything in order; the same copy opens from the app, also without internet.

Installation

Takes about a minute and needs no administrator rights.

  1. Download the installer

    The file Nutka-Setup.exe from the app page (the “Download” button) or from GitHub. You can also copy it from someone, e.g. on a USB stick.

  2. Run it

    Click Next. Tick “Desktop shortcut” if you want an icon on the desktop. The installer speaks English when your Windows does.

  3. Finish

    Leave “Launch Nutka” ticked and click Finish. Later you open Nutka from the Start menu or the shortcut.

Windows showed a blue “Windows protected your PC” box? Click More info, then Run anyway. This warning appears for every new app without a paid code-signing certificate and doesn't mean the file is dangerous. Nutka's code is fully public on GitHub.

Requirements: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), about 400 MB of disk space, internet for searching and downloading music.

Searching and listening

Type a track title or an artist and press Enter or Search. Results come from YouTube Music, so they land on the official versions of tracks. The Songs / Albums switch decides whether you are looking for songs or whole albums.

  • A click on a result selects it, and its address appears in the “Link” field.
  • A double-click on a result downloads it as an mp3 right away.
  • ▶ Listen plays the selected track without downloading. With an album it plays the whole album in order.
  • While playing, the button turns into ■ Stop, and Next › appears next to it, which skips to the next track of the album.
  • Clicking another result while something is playing switches to the new track straight away.

Downloading mp3

Select a result or paste a link and click Download mp3. The progress bar shows the download, and the box at the bottom shows the title and finally “Done”.

Files go to the Music \ Nutka folder (C:\Users\<you>\Music\Nutka). Change the folder with the Browse… button and open it with Open folder. An album and a playlist land in their own subfolder, an album with track numbers.

The music always comes from YouTube, also with Spotify links: Nutka takes only the title, artist and cover art from Spotify and downloads the audio from YouTube. That's why you occasionally get a different version of a track, e.g. a live one. The quality is mp3 at roughly 250–270 kbps.

Spotify playlists and albums

A whole playlist or album takes three steps, and next time Nutka picks up only the new tracks.

  1. Copy the link

    In Spotify, next to the playlist or album, click … → Share → Copy link.

  2. Paste it into Nutka

    Into the search box, then Enter. After a few seconds you see the track list with tick boxes.

  3. Download selected

    Untick what you don't want (click the tick box or double-click the row) and click Download selected.

  • Every track shows its status: searching, downloading 64 %, ✓ done or ✗ error. Nutka downloads 3 tracks at a time; Stop interrupts.
  • Tracks you already have are marked already yours and unticked. Just load the playlist again to pick up the new ones.
  • If something fails, those tracks stay ticked. Click Download selected again to retry.
  • A playlist goes into a subfolder with its name, an album with track numbers. The files carry the title, artist, album and cover art from Spotify.
  • Close list goes back to normal searching.

Nutka sees only public playlists. It can't load private playlists or “Liked Songs”. Make the playlist public or copy the tracks into a public one.

Language

Nutka speaks Polish and English.

On first start the app follows your Windows language: Polish Windows gets Polish, any other gets English. The small PL / EN button in the top-right corner switches the language; Nutka restarts in the other language and remembers the choice. The guide and the installer come in both languages too.

Updates

Nothing to do, Nutka updates itself.

YouTube changes its site now and then, so at every start and every 6 hours the app quietly checks for a newer “download engine” and swaps it in. Without internet, or if the update fails, the app keeps working on the version it has.

When a new version of the whole app is out, Nutka shows a window with the list of changes. Click Update now: the app downloads the new version, installs it and restarts (about a minute). Remind me later closes the window until the next start, and Skip this version stops reminding you about it.

You can check manually with the Check for updates link in the bottom-left corner of the window. Your version number is next to it.

Every release is digitally signed with the author's key. Nutka verifies the signature and the checksum before running the installer, and downloads it only from the official releases page on GitHub.

The problem keeps coming back? Prepare a report. Open the app folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Nutka), click the Explorer address bar, type cmd and press Enter, then in the black window type Nutka.exe --autotest > report.txt. The file report.txt shows what is missing. Attach it to an issue on GitHub.

Uninstalling

Go to Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find Nutka and choose Uninstall. The app is removed entirely, together with the updates it downloaded. Your mp3 files in the Music \ Nutka folder stay.
